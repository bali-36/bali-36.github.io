Philosophy

My Learning Philosophy

I believe that cybersecurity is a field where learning never stops. The threat landscape evolves constantly, new vulnerabilities are discovered daily, and attack techniques become more sophisticated. My approach to education goes beyond formal academics — it's a lifestyle of continuous curiosity and hands-on experimentation.

While my university education provides strong theoretical foundations and structured learning, I complement it with self-directed study, practical labs, and real-world projects. I believe the best way to learn security is by doing — breaking things (ethically), fixing them, and understanding why they broke in the first place.

I allocate time daily for learning something new, whether it's a new exploit technique, a security tool, or understanding how a recent major breach occurred. This dedication to continuous improvement ensures I stay relevant and effective in the fast-paced world of cybersecurity.
